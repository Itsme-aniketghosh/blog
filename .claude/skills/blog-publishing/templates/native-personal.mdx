Write it like you talk. Short sentences are fine. So are long ones when the idea
needs the room.

No heading is required for a short essay — let the prose carry it. Add `##`
sections only when the piece is long enough to need them.

You can still use a <SideNote>margin note</SideNote> for an aside, but personal
posts don't need math or code.
